About Me

Backend engineer who turns data and events into reliable, observable systems that scale with business needs.

With several years in enterprise backend development, my foundation is Java/J2EE and Spring Boot, where I design and maintain REST APIs backed by Hibernate and MySQL.

Beyond Java, I build Python services for automation and data analysis, replacing manual effort with repeatable workflows. I also craft Splunk alerts with complex SPL queries that turn raw logs into actionable insight, so issues are caught before they reach users.

On the integration side, I work with Kafka message streaming across Spring Boot and Flask services, and rely on Prometheus and Grafana to keep systems observable and measurable.

I collaborate closely with teammates and stakeholders, adapt quickly to new tools and domains, and value clean, testable code. For me, great engineering is not only about shipping features; it is about building confidence in how they run in production.
